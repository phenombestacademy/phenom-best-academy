# Phenom Best Academy — Programme Catalogue & Recognition Framework

This catalogue is a starting commercial and academic framework. Prices, durations, pass marks, content, availability and recognition labels are editable by authorised Academy administrators before publication.

## Learning delivery standard

Each structured online programme may contain:

- learner enrolment and entitlement checks;
- programme, module and lesson hierarchy;
- reading lessons and study notes;
- video, audio and external-resource lessons;
- permitted downloads such as PDFs, workbooks, manuals, templates and other learning materials;
- learner progress tracking;
- formative and final assessments;
- automatic marking and scoring where objective marking is possible;
- corrections, explanations and feedback;
- configurable pass marks;
- recorded assessment attempts;
- completion tracking;
- certificate eligibility rules;
- certificate numbering and branded completion records; and
- printable/PDF-friendly certificate layouts.

## Recognition framework

- **Non-certifying:** advisory services, consultations, resource briefings and other engagements where learning recognition is not an appropriate outcome.
- **Certificate of Participation:** used where the main evidence is participation/attendance and no competence assessment is required.
- **Certificate of Completion:** used where the learner completes the configured programme requirements; an assessment pass may be required where configured.
- **Certificate / Advanced Certificate:** used for a structured assessed learning programme with defined outcomes and a formal Academy-issued certificate title.
- **Diploma Pathway:** reserved for longer, advanced programmes that are formally partnered with an appropriately recognised awarding institution.

## Language education pathways

Minimum language programme duration: **3 months**. Standard duration options: **3, 6, 12 and 18 months**.

The current catalogue includes Yoruba, Hausa, Swahili, Zulu, Shona, Somali, Amharic, French, Spanish, German and Mandarin, with additional languages available to authorised administrators.

## Quality rule

Programme content should be reviewed before publication. Where an external credential, accreditation, regulated service or professional licence is involved, the platform must state clearly who awards or regulates that credential/service.

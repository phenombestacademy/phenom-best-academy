# Phenom Best Academy — Programme Catalogue & Recognition Framework

This catalogue is a starting commercial and academic framework. Prices, durations, pass marks, content, availability and recognition labels are editable by authorised Academy administrators before publication.

## Learning delivery standard

Each structured online programme may contain:

- learner enrolment and entitlement checks;
- programme, module and lesson hierarchy;
- reading lessons and study notes;
- video, audio and external-resource lessons;
- permitted downloads such as PDFs, workbooks, manuals, templates and other learning materials;
- learner progress tracking;
- formative and final assessments;
- automatic marking and scoring where objective marking is possible;
- corrections, explanations and feedback;
- configurable pass marks;
- recorded assessment attempts;
- completion tracking;
- certificate eligibility rules;
- certificate numbering and branded completion records; and
- printable/PDF-friendly certificate layouts.

## Recognition framework

- **Non-certifying:** advisory services, consultations, resource briefings and other engagements where learning recognition is not an appropriate outcome.
- **Certificate of Participation:** used where the main evidence is participation/attendance and no competence assessment is required.
- **Certificate of Completion:** used where the learner completes the configured programme requirements; an assessment pass may be required where configured.
- **Certificate / Advanced Certificate:** used for a structured assessed learning programme with defined outcomes and a formal Academy-issued certificate title.
- **Diploma Pathway:** reserved for longer, advanced programmes that are formally partnered with an appropriately recognised awarding institution. The Academy must not describe an external diploma as its own university/polytechnic award.

## Affiliation policy — keep partnerships limited and purposeful

Most Academy programmes should **not** require external affiliation. A partnership should be used only when it materially improves the service, provides a regulated capability, or is necessary for an external qualification/official process.

### University or polytechnic

Use only when a programme is intended to lead to an external academic award or formal academic pathway, such as the 18-month language diploma pathway.

### Professional bodies / recognised awarding providers

Use when the programme explicitly promises an external professional certification, recognised examination or third-party credential. The external provider remains responsible for its credential.

### Licensed counselling / healthcare professionals or hospitals

Use only when a client presents a matter requiring clinical, medical, psychological or other regulated professional intervention beyond the Academy's educational/guidance scope. Routine educational guidance does not require hospital affiliation.

### Study-abroad, visa and international advisory

The Academy can provide education and application-readiness support without claiming to be a government authority. Where a particular destination, institution, immigration process, regulated immigration service or official application channel requires an authorised intermediary or specialist, use the appropriate recognised provider on a case-by-case basis.

### Government and government agencies

Do not create a government affiliation merely for appearance. Use a government body only where an official process, verification, regulatory requirement, public programme or formal partnership genuinely requires it.

### NGOs and community organisations

NGO/community partnerships are useful for outreach, funded programmes, capacity building and community development, but they are not required for ordinary Academy delivery.

## Design and library services

The Digital Learning / Library ecosystem also covers practical creative and document services such as posters, flyers, social-media graphics, ID cards, branded documents, learning materials, templates and other approved digital resources. These are generally service/resource products rather than certificate programmes unless separately converted into a structured assessed learning course.

## Quality rule

Programme content should be reviewed before publication. Where an external credential, accreditation, regulated service or professional licence is involved, the platform must state clearly who awards or regulates that credential/service. Academy-issued completion certificates should never be presented as government, university, polytechnic or professional-body awards unless that external organisation formally awards them.

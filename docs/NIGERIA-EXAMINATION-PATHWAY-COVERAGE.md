# Nigeria examination and progression pathway coverage

Audited 24 September 2026. The Nigeria pathway layer covers Common Entrance/NCEE, BECE, WAEC and NECO senior certification, NABTEB, JAMB UTME/Direct Entry, Post-UTME, CAPS, IBASS and tertiary progression.

The senior private-candidate pathways are explicitly represented as:
- WAEC GCE — private candidates.
- NECO GCE / SSCE External — private candidates.

No NECO JCE pathway is represented as a current examination. The previous internal label was corrected to NECO GCE / SSCE External. Historical/legacy junior-examination references are not used as current national pathway names.

Official exam content remains link/provenance-only where rights are not established.

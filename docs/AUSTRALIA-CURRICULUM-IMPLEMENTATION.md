## Official curriculum structure
The national Australian Curriculum has three dimensions:
1. **8 discipline-based learning areas:** English; Mathematics; Science; Humanities and Social Sciences; Health and Physical Education; Languages; Technologies; The Arts.
2. **7 general capabilities:** Literacy; Numeracy; Digital Literacy; Critical and Creative Thinking; Personal and Social Capability; Ethical Understanding; Intercultural Understanding.
3. **3 cross-curriculum priorities:** Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Histories and Cultures; Asia and Australia’s Engagement with Asia; Sustainability.

The cross-curriculum priorities and general capabilities are embedded through learning-area content rather than treated as separate subjects.

## Levels and pathways
The level layer contains Foundation, Years 1–10 and Years 11–12. F–10 Version 9.0 is the principal national curriculum reference. Senior-secondary national curriculum material is retained as the applicable national reference layer; the current ACARA senior-secondary page identifies its published senior-secondary curriculum as Version 8.4, with state and territory systems responsible for local courses, assessment and certification arrangements.

## Authority and implementation rule
ACARA states that Version 9.0 is implemented by states and territories according to their own timelines and policies. The LMS therefore keeps the national curriculum authoritative while separately cataloguing jurisdictional implementation references.

## Integrity rule
Queued is **not** the same as generated, stored or published. The library will only mark a resource generated/stored/published after the corresponding material actually exists and passes the required verification. Existing records remain intact.

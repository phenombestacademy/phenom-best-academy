# Australia Curriculum Implementation

## Status
Australia is represented as a national Australian Curriculum layer with Version 9.0 explicitly preserved, alongside the existing Australian Curriculum record. State and territory implementation differences must remain distinguishable.

## Verified live database baseline
- Australian Curriculum: 13 levels, 8 subjects, 35 framework components, 440 topics and 440 asset records.
- The 440 asset records are queued production records, not completed local files.
- Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 remains separately represented in the curriculum architecture and its queued production work is preserved.
- Existing material is preserved; no existing curriculum or asset records are deleted or duplicated solely because Version 9.0 is present.

## Immediate official stock
Seven official ACARA resources are now registered as immediately accessible external library resources. They are marked as external links in stock, not as locally copied files:
1. Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 curriculum portal
2. Australian Curriculum Review 2020–2021
3. Australian Curriculum development and implementation guidance
4. Curriculum leadership resources released June 2026
5. Formative assessment resources released August 2026

The library records preserve official authority, URL, access/licence classification, retrieval status and source version. Local copies are not claimed unless actually retrieved and stored.

## Authoritative baseline
ACARA Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 is the principal national F–10 reference. State and territory authorities determine implementation timelines and local delivery. Senior secondary Australian Curriculum subjects are a common national base, while state/territory authorities integrate content into their courses and certification systems.

## Operating rule
Work in parallel: stock legitimate, immediately accessible official material now; populate production-ready material as verified curriculum data becomes available; and keep the remaining large-scale production workload queued. No queued resource is represented as generated or locally stored until it actually exists and passes IQA.

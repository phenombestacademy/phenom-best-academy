# Scotland Curriculum Implementation — Curriculum for Excellence

Status: structurally implemented in the Phenom LMS on 24 September 2026.

## Official framework
Scotland's Curriculum for Excellence (CfE) provides a coherent 3–18 curriculum. The Broad General Education has five levels: Early, First, Second, Third and Fourth. The Senior Phase covers S4–S6 and builds on BGE.

## Coverage implemented
- Early Learning and Childcare / Early Level
- Broad General Education: Early, First, Second, Third and Fourth
- Senior Phase: S4, S5, S6
- National Qualifications: National 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
- Higher
- Advanced Higher
- Post-school progression pathway
- Eight CfE curriculum areas
- Literacy/numeracy/health and wellbeing responsibilities across learning
- Gaelic and language pathways
- Senior-phase subject anchors across academic, creative, technological and vocational subjects
- SCQF and qualification progression
- National qualification assessment/examination pathway
- College, university and positive-destination progression

## Current 2026 qualification authority note
Qualifications Scotland replaced SQA as Scotland's national awarding body on 1 February 2026. Existing SQA-hosted qualification documents remain current where the official site states that they remain valid.

## Current curriculum-change note
Scotland is undertaking the Curriculum Improvement Cycle. Current CfE sources are retained as the live framework, while future curriculum changes are tracked as a separate versioned layer. Do not overwrite the current framework with draft reform material.

## Phenom resource architecture
Every curriculum topic receives the standard 14-resource pack:
lesson, study note, worked example, practice, assessment, answers, marking guide, revision, teacher guide, activities, audio script, video script, downloadable and workbook.

Each topic also receives:
- topic reconciliation job
- full resource-pack generation job
- canonical Library records
- official source/provenance alignment

Resource records are draft/queued until actual Phenom-authored content has been generated and quality-assured. They must not be represented as completed official Scottish curriculum content.

## Editorial control
Canonical curriculum Library resources remain under super-admin editorial control. Teachers can consume/select resources but do not edit the canonical source resources.

## Copyright/source rule
Official curriculum and examination documents are retained as provenance and external links unless licensing permits storage. Restricted examination papers must not be copied into the Library without the necessary rights.

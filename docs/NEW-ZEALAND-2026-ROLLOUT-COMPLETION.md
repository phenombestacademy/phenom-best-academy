# New Zealand Curriculum — Completion and 2026 Rollout Layer

## Official current position
Verified against the Ministry of Education's Tāhūrangi page updated 24 September 2026.

The National Curriculum consists of the New Zealand Curriculum (NZC) and Te Marautanga o Aotearoa (TMoA). The fully updated Years 0–10 NZC learning areas and framework are available for schools and kura to begin using in 2027. The fully updated Tau 0–10 TMoA is scheduled for release on 25 September 2026.

The implementation schedule is versioned:
- 2026: Years 0–10 English, Mathematics and Statistics, Te Reo Rangatira, Te Pāngarau.
- 2027: Years 0–8 Science, Social Sciences, Te Pūtaiao and Te Pūmanawa Tangata; Years 9–10 all learning areas/wāhanga ako.
- 2028: Year 11 updated curriculum.
- 2029: remaining Years 0–8 learning areas plus Year 12.
- 2030: Year 13.

## Senior secondary transition
From 2028, NCEA begins to be replaced by:
- Foundational Award — Year 11, 2028
- New Zealand Certificate of Education (NZCE) — Year 12, 2029
- New Zealand Advanced Certificate of Education (NZACE) — Year 13, 2030

Current NCEA and New Zealand Scholarship material remains a versioned pathway during transition.

## Quality rule
Framework anchors, database records and queued jobs are not treated as completed curriculum content. Each item must be reconciled to the current official source and pass resource/IQA checks before being marked approved.

Official material remains provenance-controlled. Phenom-created teaching and assessment resources are original resources and are not represented as official Ministry, NZQA or school examination material.

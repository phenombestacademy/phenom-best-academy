## Current statutory curriculum

The current framework covers the 12 years of compulsory education:

- Foundation Stage: P1–P2
- Key Stage 1: P3–P4
- Key Stage 2: P5–P7
- Key Stage 3: Years 8–10
- Key Stage 4: Years 11–12
- Post-16: Years 13–14

Current curriculum areas include language and literacy, mathematics and numeracy, arts, the world around us, personal development and mutual understanding, physical education, Learning for Life and Work, modern languages, environment and society, and science and technology. Religious education and the cross-curricular skills are separately represented.

The library also now carries the official Minimum Content Order reference, curriculum/assessment guidance, current RSE requirements, the KS3 CPR/AED curriculum amendment, and the Northern Ireland Curriculum Framework for Initial Teacher Education.

## Assessment

The 2026 statutory assessment policy layer is represented separately. It establishes a statutory pathway from Year 1 to Year 10, including baseline, phonics, reading-fluency, literacy, numeracy, multiplication and science assessment points.

## Qualifications and examinations

The library includes an official CCEA Regulation reference for Northern Ireland GCE and GCSE qualification criteria. Detailed qualification specifications, assessment materials and examination resources remain subject to verified source acquisition and the production queue.

Phenom-generated examination questions and marking schemes must be original and curriculum-aligned rather than reproducing copyrighted examination papers.

## Curriculum 2028

The proposed Northern Ireland Curriculum 2028 is recorded as a **proposed/reform layer**, not as current statutory curriculum. The public consultation opened in June 2026 and closes 30 September 2026; proposed phased implementation is from September 2028. Subject frameworks, capabilities and implementation guidance are therefore versioned separately and must not overwrite the current curriculum.

The official 2028 subject-framework page provides individual frameworks for Art and Design, Digital Technology, English and Drama, Geography, Home Economics, Mathematics, History, Modern Languages, Music, Physical Education, Personal Social Civic and Careers, Science, Technology and Design, plus Foundation Stage, KS1 and KS2 key-stage documents.

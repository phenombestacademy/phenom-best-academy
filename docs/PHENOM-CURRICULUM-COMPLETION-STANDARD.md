# Phenom Best Academy — Universal Curriculum Completion Standard

**Motto:** Tranquil Excellence for Global Impact

This document is a mandatory completion gate for every curriculum implemented in Phenom Best Academy. A curriculum must not be advertised as complete until every applicable official requirement has been reconciled against the Phenom LMS and Library and the required learning-resource layers have been verified.

## 1. Source and curriculum identity

For every applicable curriculum requirement, record:

- Curriculum/source reference
- Official source authority
- Source title, version/date and status
- Country/jurisdiction
- Education stage
- Key stage/year/class/grade or the jurisdiction's equivalent
- Subject/discipline/learning area
- Strand/domain/area of learning
- Topic
- Subtopic
- Knowledge statement/content requirement
- Skill/competency
- Attainment expectation/standard where applicable
- Learning outcome

Do not invent statutory requirements. Clearly distinguish statutory requirements, official non-statutory guidance, qualification/assessment requirements, and Phenom-created pedagogical organisation or enhancement.

## 2. Teaching and learning package

Every applicable topic/subtopic or requirement must have the appropriate available learning package:

- Scheme of work
- Lesson/lesson sequence
- Learner study material
- Teacher's guide
- Examples
- Worked examples where applicable
- Activities
- Workbook/workbook activities
- Practice
- Assessment
- Answers
- Marking guide/rubric where applicable
- Revision material
- Audio script and audio resource where appropriate
- Video script and video resource where appropriate
- Downloadable resource
- Progression/prerequisite links
- Related/interdisciplinary links where useful
- SEND/inclusion considerations where applicable
- Differentiation/intervention/extension guidance where appropriate

Resource formats must reflect the subject and age/stage. Not every subject requires an audio or video file, but where it is pedagogically useful it should be provided or clearly classified as pending rather than silently omitted.

## 3. Existing-content preservation

Verified existing Phenom content must be preserved. Do not replace complete, quality-checked material merely to regenerate it.

For each requirement, classify the existing material as:

- Complete — retain
- Complete but requires current-source refresh — retain and update source metadata
- Partially complete — extend
- Misaligned — correct while preserving useful content
- Duplicate — consolidate without losing useful information
- Missing — create
- Not applicable — document the reason

Older verified curriculum versions may be used as historical/reference material. New requirements and policy changes must be incorporated with current source/version metadata. Do not silently overwrite historical versions.

## 4. Quality-control completion gate

A curriculum can be labelled **COMPLETE** only when:

1. All applicable official requirements have been reconciled.
2. No unexplained curriculum gaps remain.
3. Every applicable topic/subtopic/content requirement has the appropriate learning-resource layer.
4. Existing verified resources have been preserved or deliberately versioned.
5. Source references and versions are recorded.
6. Assessment and attainment expectations are correctly represented.
7. Downloadable resources are available where required.
8. Progression and prerequisite relationships are checked.
9. SEND/inclusion considerations are present where applicable.
10. Safeguarding, welfare, policy and implementation requirements relevant to that jurisdiction/stage are represented where applicable.
11. Quality-control review has passed.
12. The LMS/library can actually surface the resulting material to authorised users.

## 5. Supervisor / audit principle

Every curriculum should have a supervisor/quality-control process that can report:

- Total official requirements identified
- Requirements mapped to Phenom
- Complete resources
- Partial resources
- Missing resources
- Current-source updates required
- Downloadable resources missing
- Assessment/answer/marking gaps
- Teacher-guide gaps
- Audio/video gaps where applicable
- Progression/prerequisite gaps
- Inclusion/SEND gaps where applicable
- Source-verification status
- Final quality-control status

The supervisor must not mark a curriculum complete simply because high-level topic records exist.

## 6. England implementation example

For England, the pathway is:

**EYFS — Early Years Foundation Stage**
- Birth to 2
- 2 to 3
- 3 to 4 / Nursery
- Reception
- EYFS Profile
- Seven areas of learning and development
- Educational programmes
- Development Matters
- Early-years assessment
- Transition into Year 1
- Safeguarding, welfare and workforce requirements

**KS1:** Years 1–2

**KS2:** Years 3–6

**KS3:** Years 7–9

**KS4:** Years 10–11

England's official statutory National Curriculum content must be reconciled at the detailed programme-of-study level, not merely represented by high-level topic containers. EYFS is treated as a separate statutory framework rather than an invented KS0.

## 7. Universal Phenom rule

This standard applies to **every applicable curriculum framework** in Phenom Best Academy, not only England or Nigeria.

The public promise is simple:

> **If Phenom says a curriculum is complete, its applicable official requirements have actually been reconciled, its learning resources are in place, and the implementation has passed quality control.**

A curriculum must remain marked **in progress** until that condition is met.

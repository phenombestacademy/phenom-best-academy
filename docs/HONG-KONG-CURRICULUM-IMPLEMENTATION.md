# Hong Kong — Education Bureau Curriculum Implementation

## Implementation checkpoint — 25 September 2026

### Official control layer
The implementation follows the Hong Kong Education Bureau curriculum framework and current 2026/27 updates. The EDB's current renewal programme includes the Values Education Curriculum Framework (2026), updated learning goals, digital education development, Primary Science and Primary Humanities, and continuing optimisation of senior secondary curricula.

Primary Science and Primary Humanities are being phased in from 2025/26 and extended to all primary levels in 2027/28. Citizenship, Economics and Society is fully implemented at secondary level in 2026/27.

### Live LMS implementation
- Curriculum: `hong-kong-edb`
- Curriculum anchors/topics: 180
- Education/pathway levels: 15 (K1-K3, P1-P6, S1-S6)
- Curriculum subject mappings: 48
- Official source records: 8
- Framework components: 10
- Official external resources in stock: 8
- Acquisition records: 8
- Core resource-pack records: 2,520 (14 resource families × 180 topics)
- Stored curriculum assets: 4,320 (24 asset types × 180 topics)
- Stored schemes of work: 180
- Detailed production jobs retained in queue: 1,080

### Core resource families
Each curriculum anchor has stored records for activities, answers, assessment, audio script, downloadable resource, lesson, marking guide, practice, revision, study note, teacher guide, video script, workbook and worked example.

### Stored curriculum assets
Each topic has a controlled LMS asset record for scheme of work, lesson plan, study note, practice set, assessment, answer/feedback, mark scheme, revision guide, teacher guide, worked examples, teaching material, educational resource, activities, workbook, audio script, video script, download pack, topic guide, differentiated resource, extension challenge, interactive activity, IQA, original examination question and simulation/virtual-lab coverage.

### Schemes of work
All 180 topic anchors have a stored scheme-of-work record. The scheme structure includes curriculum alignment/source reference, learning intention and success criteria, prior knowledge/diagnostic check, explicit teaching/modelled examples, guided and collaborative practice, independent practice, formative assessment and feedback, retrieval/reflection/consolidation, summative assessment and marking, differentiation/accessibility, extension/challenge, teaching and learning resources, home-school connection where appropriate, and teacher reflection/IQA/provenance.

### Controlled production queue
The 1,080 existing detailed jobs remain queued. They are not falsely marked complete. They cover deeper source-grounded production/enrichment, including detailed topic/subtopic reconciliation, richer teaching materials, examination resources, differentiation, interactive work, multimedia and specialist simulation/virtual-lab production where appropriate.

### Integrity and permissions
- Existing curriculum records were preserved.
- No duplicate replacement of existing records was performed.
- Teachers remain unable to edit curriculum-library assets; super-admin control is retained.
- Stored LMS assets are structured curriculum records; this checkpoint does not falsely claim that every resource is a separate binary PDF/DOCX/video file in object storage.
- Queued detailed production remains visible and auditable.

# Canada Curriculum Implementation — Pan-Canadian + 13 Jurisdictions

Status: **FOUNDATION + SCHEME LAYER IMPLEMENTED / DETAILED RESOURCE PRODUCTION QUEUED**

Canada is not represented as a single national K–12 syllabus. Education is the responsibility of 10 provinces and 3 territories, with CMEC providing pan-Canadian coordination and resources. Jurisdiction-specific curricula, assessment, graduation and pathway requirements remain authoritative.

## Scheme-of-work standard

Every Canadian anchor topic has a published Phenom scheme covering:

1. curriculum alignment and jurisdictional source control;
2. learning outcomes;
3. diagnostic/prior-knowledge activity;
4. explicit teaching/modelled examples;
5. guided and collaborative practice;
6. independent practice and formative checks;
7. retrieval, reflection and consolidation;
8. summative assessment and feedback;
9. teaching/learning approaches;
10. official and approved resource use;
11. differentiation, accessibility and extension;
12. quality-assurance/provenance rules.

Teachers do not edit these library assets. They are published as non-editable library resources; super-admin remains the authoritative editor.

## Jurisdiction coverage

The implementation preserves the 13 separate systems:

1. Alberta
2. British Columbia
3. Manitoba
4. New Brunswick
5. Newfoundland and Labrador
6. Nova Scotia
7. Ontario
8. Prince Edward Island
9. Quebec
10. Saskatchewan
11. Northwest Territories
12. Nunavut
13. Yukon

Special structures such as New Brunswick's anglophone/francophone sectors, Quebec's distinct programme structure, Nunavut's Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit-grounded curriculum, and NWT's adapted curriculum/assessment transition remain jurisdiction-specific rather than being flattened into a fictional national syllabus.

## Integrity rule

Existing material is preserved. Nothing is deleted merely because another version or jurisdictional source is added. No resource is labelled generated/in stock until it actually exists and passes the relevant IQA gate. Queued jobs remain explicitly queued.

## Official baseline

CMEC states that Canada has no federal department of education or integrated national education system; education is the responsibility of the 13 provinces and territories. CMEC's Student Transfer Guide provides jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction secondary curriculum, assessment, graduation and pathway information.

Official baseline: https://www.cmec.ca/680/Overview.html

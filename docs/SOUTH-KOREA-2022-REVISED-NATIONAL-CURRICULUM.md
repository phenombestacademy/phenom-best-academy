# South Korea — 2022 Revised National Curriculum

Implemented as a provenance-controlled curriculum layer in Supabase.

## Verified foundation
- 13 LMS levels: Elementary 1–6, Middle 1–3, High 1–3, Special Education
- 33 subject-stage mappings
- 3 official source records
- 10 framework/pathway components
- 3 official resources catalogued as external-link stock
- 3 acquisition records
- 132 subject-stage curriculum anchors
- 2,508 queued production jobs

## Official basis
The Ministry of Education's 2022 Revised National Curriculum is the authoritative base. NCIC provides the official curriculum text inventory and achievement-standard access. Later official amendments are retained as versioned records rather than overwriting the base curriculum.

## Production queue
Each curriculum anchor has queued work for scheme of work, lessons, study notes, practice, assessment, revision, worked examples, teacher guides, worksheets, examination questions, audio/video scripts, interactive activities, simulations/virtual labs where appropriate, differentiation, feedback and IQA/provenance.

Queued is not completed. An item is only marked complete when an actual resource asset exists and passes the applicable provenance/IQA checks.

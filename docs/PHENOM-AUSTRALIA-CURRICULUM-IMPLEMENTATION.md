# Phenom Best LMS — Australia Curriculum Implementation Standard

## Scope

The Australian curriculum implementation is an international education layer, not a Foundation–Year 10 topic catalogue alone.

### National curriculum

- Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 — Foundation to Year 10
- English
- Mathematics
- Science
- Health and Physical Education
- Humanities and Social Sciences: HASS F–6, Civics and Citizenship, Economics and Business, Geography, History
- The Arts: Dance, Drama, Media Arts, Music, Visual Arts
- Technologies: Design and Technologies, Digital Technologies
- Languages and applicable language-specific/framework pathways
- General capabilities
- Cross-curriculum priorities

### Senior secondary and jurisdictional pathways

Represent each jurisdiction under its own authority and terminology, without flattening state/territory systems into ACARA:

- NSW Higher School Certificate (HSC)
- Victoria Victorian Certificate of Education (VCE), including VCE Vocational Major
- Queensland Queensland Certificate of Education (QCE)
- Western Australia Western Australian Certificate of Education (WACE)
- South Australia South Australian Certificate of Education (SACE)
- Tasmania Tasmanian Certificate of Education (TCE)
- Northern Territory senior secondary certification and assessment arrangements
- ACT senior secondary certification and assessment arrangements

Also represent ATAR and relevant tertiary admission pathways, NAPLAN, VET/TAFE pathways, senior-secondary assessment, examination specifications, subject/syllabus documents and teacher support material where officially available and legally storable/redistributable.

## Canonical content hierarchy

`Authority → framework/version → jurisdiction/pathway → level/year/band → subject → syllabus/domain/strand → topic/subtopic → content requirement → achievement/assessment expectation → resource`

Every official source must retain its authority, URL, version, retrieval date, licence/access class and content hash where a physical copy is legally stored.

## Learning-resource layer

Each applicable curriculum element should resolve to:

- scheme/work programme
- lesson sequence
- learner study material
- teacher guide
- examples and worked examples
- activities/practical work
- workbook/practice
- assessment and answers
- marking guide/rubric where applicable
- revision
- examination preparation
- audio/video scripts or media where appropriate
- downloadable human-readable formats
- progression/prerequisite relationships
- differentiation, intervention, extension and inclusion guidance where applicable

Official material and Phenom-authored/generated material must remain explicitly distinguished.

## Completion rule

A curriculum is not marked complete merely because framework records, topic seeds or generation jobs exist. Completion requires reconciliation of the applicable official content, assessment requirements and resource layer; successful physical/canonical Library storage for material that is authorised to be stored; human-readable access; relationship integrity; duplicate/orphan checks; and quality-control verification.

Queued generation is an implementation state, not completion. Failed, missing or unverified jobs remain open until resolved.

## Sources

Primary national source: https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/

State/territory assessment and certification authorities must be sourced directly from their current official publications. Current versions must be versioned rather than silently replacing historical records.

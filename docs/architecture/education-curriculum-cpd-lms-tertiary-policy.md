# Phenom Best Academy — Education, Curriculum, CPD, LMS and Tertiary Architecture Policy

## 1. Governing principle

Phenom Best Academy builds the learning architecture **before** an external university, polytechnic, professional body, regulator or other awarding authority is formally attached.

The prebuilt system may contain:
- curriculum maps;
- programme structures;
- levels and progression;
- topic hierarchies;
- lessons;
- assessments and CBTs;
- resource-library structures;
- downloadable learning materials;
- schedules and workload plans;
- learning outcomes and competency maps;
- certificates/credential workflows;
- student-support and LMS workflows.

This prebuilding does **not** mean that Phenom Best Academy is claiming to be the external awarding institution.

## 2. Curriculum authority rule

Where an authoritative institution publishes a curriculum, Phenom uses that curriculum as the reference source. Examples include national curriculum authorities, university regulators, polytechnic/TVET authorities, Cambridge International and other recognised awarding bodies.

Where the authority provides a framework, subject specification, syllabus, benchmark or minimum academic standard rather than complete teaching materials, Phenom may build the complete instructional resources against those verified requirements.

Generated lessons and resources must never be described as the authority's own material unless permission exists.

## 3. Education hierarchy

The LMS hierarchy is:

Education domain → discipline/subject → curriculum or programme → stage/level → topic/subtopic → learning outcome → course/module → lesson/activity → assessment → competency.

Education is therefore not a flat subject list. It includes disciplines such as Early Childhood Education, Mathematics Education, Primary Education, Secondary Education, Teacher Education, Educational Leadership, Educational Administration and Planning, Educational Psychology, Curriculum Studies, Educational Technology, Educational Measurement and Evaluation, Educational Guidance and Counselling, Science Education, Language Education, Social Studies Education, Special and Inclusive Education, Adult Education, Vocational and Technical Education, Arts Education and Physical and Health Education.

## 4. Curriculum stages

The architecture supports the complete pathway from nursery/early years through primary, lower secondary, upper secondary, sixth-form/advanced secondary, vocational/technical education, tertiary education, postgraduate education and professional/continuing development.

Country and curriculum selection are separate from user location. A learner may choose an applicable international curriculum regardless of where the learner lives.

## 5. Cambridge pathways

The architecture explicitly supports Cambridge Primary Checkpoint, Cambridge Lower Secondary Checkpoint, Cambridge IGCSE, Cambridge O Level, Cambridge International AS & A Level and Cambridge AICE pathways.

Phenom may provide preparation, teaching resources, practice and progress tracking. External Cambridge examinations and qualification rules remain subject to Cambridge and authorised-centre requirements.

## 6. Nigerian tertiary architecture

The system distinguishes:
- university pathways governed by the applicable National Universities Commission standards and the awarding university;
- National Diploma (ND) and Higher National Diploma (HND) pathways governed by the applicable National Board for Technical Education requirements and the awarding institution;
- professional, health and other regulated pathways requiring their specific authorities.

The current tertiary reference catalogue includes the ND programme families published in the January 2026 NBTE approved-curricula brochure. The catalogue also records which programmes are identified by NBTE as having ODFeL courseware and which require an affiliation/physical arrangement before delivery.

## 7. International tertiary architecture

The catalogue is designed to accommodate qualification patterns used in the UK, US, Australia, South Africa, India, Kenya, the European Higher Education Area and other countries.

These are architectural reference pathways. A country-level framework does not by itself make Phenom an awarding institution. Specific admission, accreditation and award requirements must be verified against the institution and regulator for the actual programme.

## 8. Polytechnic and practical-programme rule

Practical programmes are not discarded. They are classified.

A programme may be:
- directly suitable for approved online/ODFeL delivery;
- an online candidate requiring formal approval;
- affiliation-required;
- physical-component-required;
- referral/pathway only.

Where a programme requires laboratory, clinical, workshop, field, studio, industrial attachment, supervised practical or compulsory face-to-face activity, the LMS prepares the theory, simulations, videos, demonstrations, projects, alternative-to-practical learning and assessment support that can lawfully be delivered online. The compulsory physical component must be completed at an authorised institution or approved centre.

## 9. University-affiliated distance-learning model

For an affiliated university programme, Phenom Best Academy can function as the approved online learning platform and/or virtual support centre for the particular programme once the agreement is verified.

The affiliated university remains responsible for:
- admission;
- admission letter;
- official student identity/record;
- academic approval;
- official university portal where applicable;
- final examinations where required;
- official transcript;
- degree/diploma/certificate award.

Phenom Best Academy supplies the agreed online learning environment, teaching/support resources, learner services and LMS administration within the institutional agreement.

If the university provides an integrated student portal, the learner may have both:
1. the university's official student account/portal; and
2. the Phenom LMS as the delivery and support environment.

Where Phenom is designated as a centre/support location, the learner may select that centre under the university's approved arrangement. Required physical activities remain at the parent campus or another location authorised by the awarding institution.

## 10. Prebuilt university curriculum

Before affiliation is finalised, Phenom may prepare the complete programme architecture and resources against the applicable published academic standards.

The university can then:
- approve the prepared structure;
- request one or more adjustments;
- replace or add content;
- change sequencing;
- specify institutional assessments;
- require additional practical activities;
- approve the final delivery version.

The LMS therefore aims to present a **ready-to-review programme**, rather than waiting until affiliation to begin creating the academic structure.

## 11. CPD

CPD remains a distinct catalogue layer from formal academic education.

CPD hierarchy:

CPD sector/domain → discipline/profession → programme → duration/workload → level → topic → resource → assessment → completion → credential.

Durations are based on programme workload and may be provisional until a professional body or external authority confirms a formal award structure.

A CPD programme can be 3, 6, 12, 18, 24, 36, 48 months or another evidence-based duration.

Sequential programmes unlock the next level only after the defined completion and assessment requirements are satisfied.

## 12. Certificate and award rule

A Phenom direct programme may use a Phenom Best Academy credential where that credential is lawful and accurately described.

An external award must identify the actual awarding institution/body.

An affiliated university degree must be represented as the university's degree, not a Phenom degree.

No public page may imply accreditation, affiliation, licensing, government approval or university awarding status until the relevant agreement/approval has been verified.

## 13. Learning-resource rule

Programme resources are designed to be available before learner enrolment where possible.

The resource architecture supports:
- lessons;
- course notes;
- PDFs;
- e-books/documents;
- audio;
- video;
- images/diagrams;
- presentations;
- worked examples;
- assignments;
- quizzes;
- CBTs;
- projects;
- alternative-to-practical activities;
- revision materials;
- teacher/facilitator guides;
- assessment rubrics;
- certificates and verification workflows.

Resources are mapped to the relevant curriculum/programme/topic and are stored as reusable assets rather than generated from scratch on every learner visit.

## 14. AI and content-generation rule

Phenom Best Core may prepare educational resources from verified curriculum requirements. It must not invent an official curriculum or falsely attribute generated content to a regulator, university, examination board or professional body.

AI/provider/model identities remain internal.

## 15. Source and verification rule

Every authoritative curriculum import records its source, version/date, extraction status and verification state.

Pending material is never displayed to learners as verified official curriculum.

Where an official source is unavailable, the architecture may create a clearly labelled reference/provisional programme structure, but it must not be presented as an official curriculum.

## 16. Final implementation objective

The finished Phenom Best Academy platform should allow the user to move naturally through:

Education/CPD → category → discipline → curriculum/programme → qualification/stage → level → topic → lesson → activity → assessment → competency → completion → credential.

For an affiliated tertiary programme, the same learner experience is delivered through Phenom while the verified external institution retains the authority assigned to it.

This policy governs the database architecture, LMS catalogue, curriculum ingestion, programme catalogue, CPD progression and future institutional-affiliation workflows.

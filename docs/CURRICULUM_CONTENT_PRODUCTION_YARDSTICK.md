# Phenom Best LMS — Curriculum Content Production Yardstick

**Status:** Standing production standard  
**Applies to:** Curriculum, LMS and CPD content production  
**Priority:** Nigeria-first expansion, followed by the wider global curriculum corpus

## 1. The 17-part resource package

Every curriculum topic, unit or CPD learning item that Phenom Best Academy develops into learner-ready content should be worked against this yardstick:

1. Curriculum / programme
2. Level
3. Subject / course
4. Topic / unit
5. Learning objectives / outcomes
6. Lesson
7. Study notes
8. Worked / mock example
9. Practice set
10. Assessment
11. Revision guide
12. Teacher / lecturer guide
13. Audio / studio script
14. Video script
15. Downloadable learner resource
16. Competency / application activity
17. Completion / progress linkage

## 2. Authority and verification rule

Official curriculum structures, topic titles, learning outcomes and other authority-controlled information must be source-verified before being presented as official.

Phenom Best Academy-created lessons, notes, examples, practices, assessments, revision materials, guides, scripts, downloadable resources and application activities may be developed from verified curriculum alignment, but they must not be represented as official government, examination-board, university, college or regulatory-authority material.

Unverified or placeholder curriculum records must not be silently promoted to verified status.

## 3. Nigeria-first authority map

For the Nigerian expansion, the relevant authority must be identified by education domain rather than treating the entire education system as one NERDC corpus:

- Early years / basic / secondary curriculum: use the applicable NERDC / Federal Ministry of Education curriculum and current official framework/source material.
- Universities: use the applicable NUC programme and discipline frameworks, including CCMAS and institution/programme-specific structures where appropriate.
- Colleges of Education: use applicable NCCE-approved standards and distinguish current approved standards from frameworks still undergoing review.
- Polytechnics / TVET: use applicable NBTE curriculum, course specifications, National Occupational Standards and related approved materials.
- Other regulated professional programmes: identify the relevant statutory/professional authority where applicable.

## 4. Current production principle

The goal is not merely to create a catalogue of curriculum names. The goal is to build a usable learning corpus in which verified curriculum structure can lead to a complete learner resource package.

The production chain is:

**Curriculum → Level → Subject/Course → Topic/Unit → Learning Outcomes → Lesson → Study Material → Worked Example → Practice → Assessment → Revision → Teacher/Lecturer Guide → Audio/Studio Script → Video Script → Downloadable Resource → Competency/Application → Completion/Progress**

## 5. Public-learning safety

Learners should only be exposed to content that has passed the applicable verification/publication rules. Source-pending records are not to be treated as completed curriculum content.

Where source material is unavailable, the system should preserve the distinction between:
- verified authority structure;
- Academy-created instructional content;
- source-pending / awaiting verification.

## 6. Nigeria-first expansion target

The Nigerian corpus is not considered complete merely because an initial verified batch exists. Expansion should continue across the applicable education stages and sectors, including early years, primary, JSS, SSS, university, college of education and polytechnic/TVET pathways.

Completion claims must be based on actual verified coverage and populated resources, not assumptions.

## 7. Current database checkpoint

At the time this standard was recorded:
- 19 curriculum records exist in the curriculum catalogue.
- 654 curriculum topic records exist in the database across the broader catalogue.
- 76 topics are the current verified-content batch used for the expanded resource corpus.
- 760 resource-pack records exist across 10 resource types for those verified topics.
- 40 curriculum content-production jobs exist in the production queue.

These figures are a checkpoint, not a declaration that the Nigerian curriculum corpus is complete.

## 8. Non-negotiable quality rule

**Never fabricate official curriculum content to make the database look complete.**

Build outward from verified source structures, then develop the full Academy-created learning package against the 17-part yardstick.

# Phenom Best LMS — Curriculum Content Standard

## NERDC Secondary Curriculum 2025

The Phenom Best LMS curriculum engine uses the supplied NEW NERDC SCHEME, 2025.pdf as the source for the current Nigerian secondary-school ingestion scope.

### Scope
- JSS1
- JSS2
- JSS3
- SS1
- SS2
- SS3

### Required curriculum hierarchy
Every curriculum record must preserve the source structure and be placed under the correct:
1. Level
2. Subject
3. Term
4. Week/sequence
5. Topic
6. Subtopic or source-derived content
7. Source/page reference

### Required learning-content structure
For every applicable topic, Phenom Best LMS is to provide or provision:
- Learning materials
- Learning objectives
- Expected learning outcomes
- Lesson materials
- Study notes
- Clear explanation
- Worked examples where applicable
- Practice activities
- Computer-Based Test (CBT) assessment
- Answer key
- Revision materials
- Teacher guide
- Audio-script provision
- Video-script provision
- Downloadable-resource provision

### Assessment rule
Objective CBT items are automatically scored from the stored answer key so the learner can receive immediate results/feedback and authorised teaching staff can receive the attempt/result signal. The Question Bank must support both teacher-authored questions and professionally prepared questions available through the Phenom Best LMS Question Bank/Marketplace.

### Source integrity
The source scheme is authoritative for the imported topic/sequence/content. Model-generated learning resources must remain curriculum-bound and must not silently invent, remove, rename, or reorder source topics.

### Future curriculum replacement
Existing Nursery, Pre-Primary, Primary, Technical Education and tertiary curriculum records are retained. They are not to be deleted merely because their replacement source materials are still being collected. When authoritative source materials are supplied, the corresponding curriculum records can be reconciled and replaced without disturbing the secondary curriculum.

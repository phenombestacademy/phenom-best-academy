# Cambridge Curriculum Completion Gate — 24 September 2026

## Non-negotiable rule

Phenom must not declare the Cambridge curriculum complete merely because topic rows, resource-pack specifications, asset jobs, or source links exist.

A Cambridge record is complete only when the authoritative curriculum/syllabus requirement has been extracted, independently source-verified, mapped to the correct record, resourced, assessed, quality-checked, and has no unresolved production work.

## Current verified position

The current Cambridge database contains:
- 378 active curriculum topic/subject records.
- 5,292 resource-pack records.
- 1,890 requirement-mapping gates (five gates per active record).
- 53 completed asset jobs.
- 5,503 queued/active asset jobs.
- 0 verified requirement mappings.

Therefore Cambridge is **NOT COMPLETE** and must not be presented as complete.

## Important source distinction

Cambridge Primary and Cambridge Lower Secondary curriculum frameworks provide learning objectives for each stage. Cambridge IGCSE, O Level and International AS & A Level are organised primarily through subject-specific syllabuses and their assessment requirements. Cambridge confirms that its syllabus content is updated over time, so source version and examination/teaching year must remain part of the mapping record.

The number 1,819 must not be described as an official Cambridge-published objective count unless an authoritative Cambridge source explicitly establishes that count. The current Phenom count of 1,890 is a database mapping-gate count, not an official Cambridge objective count.

## Add-don't-delete rule

Existing useful Cambridge content must be preserved. A newer source may supersede an older source only where it is demonstrably more relevant/current and no useful information is lost. Otherwise the newer source is added and the older source remains retained.

## Final release gate

Cambridge may be released as COMPLETE only when:
1. Every applicable authoritative objective/content requirement is extracted.
2. Every mapping is source-verified.
3. Every syllabus/framework version is recorded.
4. Every active record has the required resource set.
5. Required physical assets exist or an approved generation job can produce them.
6. Assessment, answers and marking guidance are present where applicable.
7. Teacher, learner, revision and implementation resources are present where applicable.
8. Progression/prerequisites and inclusion requirements are mapped where applicable.
9. Quality-control status is recorded as complete.
10. There are no queued/processing jobs remaining for the release scope.
11. Restricted third-party materials are not copied without the required rights; they are catalogued/linked or replaced with original Phenom-authored equivalents where appropriate.
12. The completion audit returns COMPLETE.

Until every gate passes, the platform must continue to show Cambridge as in progress rather than complete.

## Source authority

Primary source authority is Cambridge International's current official curriculum and qualification documentation. Third-party resources may support teaching-resource discovery, but they do not replace Cambridge's authoritative curriculum/syllabus source.

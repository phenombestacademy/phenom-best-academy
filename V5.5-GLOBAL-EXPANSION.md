# Phenom Best Academy V5.5 — Global Expansion

V5.5 expands the platform's global localisation and school curriculum catalogue.

## Localisation

The language selector now supports a broad global catalogue including Nigerian Pidgin, French, Spanish, Chinese, Portuguese, Arabic, German, Italian, Russian, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Kiswahili (Kenya/East Africa and Tanzania), Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Korean, Japanese, Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Persian, Hebrew, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, Vietnamese, Filipino, Ukrainian, Romanian, Czech, Greek, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, Hungarian, Amharic, Somali, isiZulu and isiXhosa, plus English regional variants.

The UI translation service batches hard-coded rendered React text and uses server-side AI providers. Provider credentials are never stored in frontend source code. If no provider secret is configured, English is retained as the safe fallback.

## Curriculum catalogue

The catalogue includes national and international frameworks with official-source provenance for NERDC Nigeria, England National Curriculum, Cambridge International, US Common Core/state-aligned standards, Australia, India/NCF-CBSE aligned, Singapore MOE, Kenya CBC, South Africa CAPS, Ghana SBC/Common Core, Rwanda CBC, New Zealand, Japan MEXT, China compulsory-education standards, Brazil BNCC, International Baccalaureate, France, UAE and Ireland.

A school can select a maximum of three frameworks. The country selection provides an automatic starting suggestion; the school can then add compatible frameworks such as Nigerian + British or a national framework + Cambridge/IB, subject to the three-framework limit and the applicable rules of the curriculum providers.

Curriculum references are not presented as invented Phenom standards. Official source links are retained, and the platform should refresh references when the authoritative body publishes changes.
